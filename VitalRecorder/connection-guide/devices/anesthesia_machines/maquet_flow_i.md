## Connection Steps

1. Attach a **null modem adapter (M/F)** to the **DB-9 serial port** on the lower right of the rear panel.

   <img src="../hardware_images/maquet_flow_i_1.png" width="450" alt="Flow-i rear panel with the DB-9 serial port outlined in red, next to a network connector">
2. Connect a **direct serial cable** between the null modem adapter and the PC's USB-Serial converter.

## Device Configuration

No changes to the machine settings are required.

## Vital Recorder Setup

Add the device as **`Flow-i`** and select the PC serial port used for the connection.
