> **Note:** This RS-232 connection supports numeric data only. To record waveforms, use the ANALOG OUT port on a TRAM-RAC 4A with an analog-to-digital converter (ADC).

| Cable | Adapter | Port | VR Device Name |
|-------|---------|------|---------------- |
| direct serial cable | None | **RS-232 1** | `Solar8000` |

## Connection Steps

1. Connect the direct serial cable to the port labeled RS-232 1.

   <img src="../hardware_images/ge_solar8000_1.png" width="450" alt="Close-up of the Solar 8000 rear connector panel; a red circle marks the 9-pin D-type female socket under the silk-screened label RS-232 1, with the labels DFP Vid 1 and VGA Vid 1 alongside and a blue VGA cable plugged into the connector below">

## Device Configuration

No changes to the monitor settings are required.

## Vital Recorder Setup

- Add the device as **`Solar8000`** and select the PC serial port used for the connection.
