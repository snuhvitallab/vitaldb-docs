> **Note:** Protocol: **GE S5 Computer Interface** (the Datex DRI protocol, shared with the CARESCAPE Bx50 and the S/5 monitors). The B40/B20 needs a custom 9-pin serial cable with **pin 4 physically removed** — a standard off-the-shelf cable will not work.

| Cable | Adapter | Port | VR Device Name |
|-------|---------|------|----------------|
| 9-pin serial cable with **pin 4 physically removed** | None | 9-pin serial port on the rear panel | `Bx50` |

> ⚠️ **Pin 4 must be left unconnected.** With pin 4 wired through, the monitor does not communicate and Vital Recorder shows an open port with no data. No Null Modem adapter is used — the pin-4 modification is the only thing that makes this cable special.

## Connection Steps

1. Buy or build a 9-pin serial cable with **pin 4 removed**. The simplest route is to buy a normal DB-9 cable and pull the pin 4 contact out of the connector shell with a pin-extraction tool; alternatively, order the cable from a cable shop with pin 4 specified as "no connect". Label the cable — it is indistinguishable from a standard one by eye.

2. Plug the cable into the **9-pin serial port** on the rear of the monitor. On both the B40 and the B20 it is on the lower connector strip, beside the network jack and next to the AC power inlet.

   <img src="../hardware_images/ge_b40_b20_1.png" width="450" alt="Two rear-panel photographs side by side, captioned B40 back view and B20 back view; on each, a red circle marks the 9-pin D-type serial connector on the lower connector strip, beside the network jack and next to the AC power inlet">

3. Connect the other end to the PC through a USB-Serial converter.

   ```
   B40/B20 9-pin serial port -- 9-pin cable, pin 4 removed -- USB-Serial (FTDI) -- PC
   ```

## Device Configuration

No service-menu change is normally required: the monitor emits the S/5 stream on the serial port as delivered. If nothing arrives, verify the cable first (pin 4) before asking the GE engineer to check the monitor's interface settings.

- Serial: **9600 baud**, per the Vital Recorder supported-device list for this family.

## Vital Recorder Setup

- Add the device in Vital Recorder as **GE :: Bx50**; in `vr.conf` the type is `Bx50` — the B40/B20 shares the Bx50 driver because both speak the S/5 Computer Interface.
- Default waveforms are `ECG1, PLETH, IABP1, CO2, AWP`; request others with `wavs=` — see [Configuration Guide → S5 / Datex Device Settings](../../../Configuration_Guide.md#s5--datex-device-settings-ge-solar--bx50--b1x5m--canvas). Invasive arterial pressure is `IABP1`, not `ART` or `INVP1`.

## Notes

- **Handshaking.** Like the other S/5-protocol GE monitors, the B40/B20 link is more reliable with a USB-Serial converter that carries DTR and RTS (FTDI recommended). Avoid PL2303-based converters, which have proved unstable in the field.
- **Older B20/B40 units** may not expose the serial port at all without GE's multi-I/O adapter fitted. If the rear panel has no 9-pin connector in the position shown above, check with biomedical engineering whether the interface option is installed.
- The **CARESCAPE B450/B650/B850** is a different device despite sharing the `Bx50` type name — it connects through a USB port with an ATEN UC-232A and a Null Modem adapter. See [GE CARESCAPE B850 / B650 / B450](ge_carescape.md).
