> **Note:** Protocol: **GE S5 Computer Interface** (the Datex DRI protocol, shared with the CARESCAPE Bx50 and the S/5 monitors). The B40/B20 needs a custom 9-pin serial cable with **pin 4 physically removed** — a standard off-the-shelf cable will not work.

| Cable | Adapter | Port | VR Device Name |
|-------|---------|------|----------------|
| 9-pin serial cable with **pin 4 physically removed** | **Null Modem adapter (F/F)** (at the PC end) | 9-pin serial port on the rear panel | `Bx50` |

## Connection Requirements

**Pin 4 must be left unconnected.** With pin 4 wired through, the monitor does not communicate and Vital Recorder shows an open port with no data. A **Null Modem adapter (F/F)** is fitted between the cable and the PC-side converter.

## Connection Steps

1. Buy or build a 9-pin serial cable with **pin 4 removed**. The simplest route is to buy a normal DB-9 cable and pull the pin 4 contact out of the connector shell with a pin-extraction tool; alternatively, order the cable from a cable shop with pin 4 specified as "no connect". Label the cable — it is indistinguishable from a standard one by eye.

2. Plug the cable into the **9-pin serial port** on the rear of the monitor. On both the B40 and the B20 it is on the lower connector strip, beside the network jack and next to the AC power inlet.

   <img src="../hardware_images/ge_b40_b20_1.png" width="450" alt="Two rear-panel photographs side by side, captioned B40 back view and B20 back view; on each, a red circle marks the 9-pin D-type serial connector on the lower connector strip, beside the network jack and next to the AC power inlet">

3. Attach a **Null Modem adapter (F/F)** to the cable's PC-side end, then connect it to the PC through a USB-Serial converter.

   ```
   B40/B20 9-pin serial port -- 9-pin cable, pin 4 removed -- Null Modem adapter (F/F) -- USB-Serial (FTDI) -- PC
   ```

## Device Configuration

No service-menu change is required, and **no baud rate has to be set** — the monitor emits the S/5 stream on the serial port as delivered and Vital Recorder opens the port with the S/5 Computer Interface settings itself. If nothing arrives, verify the cable first (pin 4) before asking the GE engineer to check the monitor's interface settings.

## Vital Recorder Setup

- Add the device in Vital Recorder as **GE :: Bx50**; in `vr.conf` the type is `Bx50` — the B40/B20 shares the Bx50 driver because both speak the S/5 Computer Interface.
- Default waveforms are `ECG1, PLETH, IABP1, CO2, AWP`; request others with `wavs=` — see [Configuration Guide → S5 / Datex Device Settings](../../../Configuration_Guide.md#s5--datex-device-settings-ge-solar--bx50--b1x5m--canvas). Invasive arterial pressure is `IABP1`, not `ART` or `INVP1`.

## Troubleshooting

- **No 9-pin connector on the rear panel in the position shown above.** Older B20/B40 units may not expose the serial port at all without GE's multi-I/O adapter fitted — check with biomedical engineering whether the interface option is installed.
- **The link drops intermittently.** Use an FTDI-based USB-Serial converter on the PC side.

## Notes

- **Null Modem adapter (F/F).** If a pin-4 cable *without* the adapter is found working on a unit, record it.
- The **CARESCAPE B450/B650/B850** is a different device despite sharing the `Bx50` type name — it connects through a USB port with a version-specific USB-Serial converter and a Null Modem adapter. See [GE CARESCAPE B850 / B650 / B450](ge_carescape.md).
